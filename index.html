How cashback works

Four simple stages, nothing hidden

From the moment you choose an offer to the moment the money reaches you.

Eligibility

A completed purchase on an offer we've confirmed with you. Cancelled or returned orders don't count.

Tracking

Send your order ID through our contact form. We reply with a claim reference you can use to check its status.

Approval

We check the purchase. If it meets the conditions, your claim is approved. If not, we tell you why.

Withdrawal

Withdraw your approved cashback by UPI, bank transfer or vouchers, whichever you prefer.
