TRAI DLT services

Help with registering on a telecom operator's DLT platform, so your business SMS meets TRAI's rules. Fees are quoted to you before we begin.

TRAI DLT

Entity registration

We help you register your business as a Principal Entity on a telecom operator's DLT platform, the first step before you can send commercial SMS in India.

  • You provide the business documents the operator asks for.
  • Approval is decided by the operator, not by Senddora.
  • We confirm our fee with you before any work starts.

TRAI DLT

Header (Sender ID) registration

We help you choose and register the sender IDs (headers) your customers will see on your SMS, in the category that fits each message.

  • Headers must relate to your registered business name or brand.
  • The operator may reject a header. We help you revise and resubmit it.
  • We confirm our fee with you before any work starts.
