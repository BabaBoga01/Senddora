Who we are

About Senddora

A calmer, clearer way to earn back on what you spend, and a straightforward partner for businesses that send SMS.

What we do

Senddora does two things. For shoppers, we offer cashback of up to 60% on selected offers, paid out by UPI, bank transfer or vouchers. For businesses, we help with registration on telecom operators' DLT platforms, which TRAI requires before you can send commercial SMS in India.

Both come from the same idea: things that involve your money or your business should be explained clearly, done carefully and never wrapped in confusing fine print.

How we work

Our principles

Clarity first

Every offer shows its conditions next to it, and we confirm your rate before you buy.

Privacy by default

We collect only what's needed to handle your claim or service, and we never sell personal data.

Honest about limits

We tell you what we can and can't promise. DLT approvals, for example, are decided by telecom operators.
