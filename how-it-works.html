How it works

Cashback with Senddora runs in five clear steps. Here's exactly what happens, and what we need from you.

Cashback, start to finish

Check you're eligible

Choose an offer on our offers page and ask us about it. We confirm the cashback rate (up to 60%) in writing before you buy. You must be 18 or older and living in India.

Shop and keep your order details

Complete your purchase as agreed. Keep your order ID, the order date and the name of the store or website. These are what we use to verify your purchase.

Submit your claim and track it

Send your order details using the cashback claim form. We reply by email with a claim reference. Quote it any time you want an update, and we'll tell you its current status:

Pending Approved Paid Declined

Verification and approval

We check that the purchase was completed and meets the offer's conditions. Approved claims move to Approved. If a claim is declined, for example because the order was cancelled or returned, we explain why by email.

Withdraw your cashback

Send a withdrawal request with your claim reference and choose how you'd like to be paid:

UPI Bank transfer Vouchers

We confirm your payout details by email. We will never ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, card number or passwords.

TRAI DLT services

Businesses in India that send commercial SMS must register on a telecom operator's DLT platform. Here's how we work with you.

Tell us what you need

Use the DLT enquiry form to tell us about your business and which registrations you need.

Get a clear quote

We confirm the scope and our fee in writing before any work starts. Any charges set by the operator are shown separately.

Prepare and submit

You share the documents the operator requires. We prepare your entity, header, content and consent details and help you submit them.

Operator review

The operator reviews and decides on each registration. If something is rejected, we help you revise and resubmit it as agreed in your quote.

Common questions

Is every offer 60% cashback?

No. 60% is the highest rate. Each offer has its own rate, and we confirm it with you in writing before you buy.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You claim cashback and request withdrawals through our contact form or by email at support@senddora.in. Your claim reference keeps track of everything.

Why was my cashback declined?

The most common reasons are a cancelled, returned or refunded order, missing order details, or a purchase that wasn't on a confirmed offer. We always tell you the reason. See our Cashback Policy.

Can Senddora guarantee DLT approval?

No. Each telecom operator decides on DLT registrations. We help you prepare and submit correct details, and help you fix anything that's rejected.

How do I stay safe?

Only trust messages from support@senddora.in. We never ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, card number or passwords, and we never ask you to pay a fee to receive cashback.
